# My Digital Guardian

A free personal cyber hygiene tool that helps everyday people see how safe their digital life is — and shows them exactly what to do about it.

No technical background needed. Nothing to install. No accounts to link.

---

## What it does

| Step | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| **Learn** | Plain-language cards cover the basics: passwords, phones and computers, home network, accounts, and social media privacy. |
| **Check** | Short questions about your current habits. |
| **Score** | A simple score showing where you stand. |
| **Act** | Concrete, step-by-step actions — not vague advice. |

## Who it's for

Anyone who isn't a security expert and wants a straight answer about whether they're protected.

## What makes it different

- **Teaching comes first.** You can learn everything useful without entering a single detail about your devices or accounts.
- **Tracking is optional.** Adding your own devices and accounts just makes the score more personal.
- **Verified steps.** Every instruction is checked against the platform's own official documentation, so the steps match what you actually see on screen.
- **Social media taken seriously.** Includes what happens when you post personal information, and what posting about children means for them long term — a profile built on a child, over a lifetime, without their knowledge or consent.
